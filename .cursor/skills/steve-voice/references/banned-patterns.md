# Banned patterns for steve-voice

If any of these appear in a draft you produced, revise before delivery. Aligns with teacher-foundation and Text-Humanizer signals.

## Banned openers and closers

- In today's... / In a world where... / In the rapidly evolving...
- Have you ever wondered... / Let's be honest... / Here's the thing... (as stock filler)
- Let's unpack... / Whether you're a beginner or an expert...
- In conclusion / To summarize / Ultimately / At the end of the day / Moving forward
- The path forward is clear / Thoughts? / What do you think? (fake engagement)

## Banned formula shapes

- X plays a crucial/critical/important/vital/pivotal role in shaping Y
- Not only X, but also Y
- From X to Y as empty range rhetoric
- That's not X. It's Y. / It's not just about X. It's about Y.
- This highlights/underscores/showcases...
- Stacked three-part parallel lists used for fake completeness

## Banned vocabulary (non-exhaustive)

delve, unpack, lean in/into, deep dive, game-changer, leverage, unlock, foster, empower, navigate (metaphorical), multifaceted, holistic, tapestry, paradigm, synergy, cutting-edge, impactful, transformative, passionate about, thrilled to, honored to, space (holding/creating space), assimilation pathway, do life together

## Banned hedging / empty intensifiers (when doing no work)

ensuring, highlights, facilitates, it's worth noting that, significantly, fundamentally, essentially, ultimately (as throat-clearing)

## Rhythm failures

- Three or more consecutive sentences the same length and structure
- Every paragraph the same shape (claim → explanation → tidy bow)
- Bullet-shaped thinking forced into essay form without spoken connective tissue
- Compliment-sandwich openings that delay the real point

## Punctuation

- Em dashes or en dashes used as rhetorical bridges
- Ellipsis abuse for fake drama...
- Excessive exclamation marks

## Content failures specific to Steve

- Invented personal anecdotes
- Softening Reformed convictions to sound broadly evangelical
- Generic "be more spiritual" applications
- Dropping an opening frame and never returning to it
- Quoting Scripture inaccurately or without reference
